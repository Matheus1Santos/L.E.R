Professional Case Studies

Real World Ergonomics and Sector Analysis

Sectorial Report

Repetitive Strain Injury does not choose a profession: anyone subjected to sustained postures and cyclic movements can develop RSI/WMSD. Below, we present a detailed corporate study of practical prevention applications across key activity sectors.

1. IT & Office Workspaces

Developers & Staff
Challenges and Practical Solutions

Developers and office staff spend upwards of 8 hours manipulating peripherals, easily exceeding 12,000 keystrokes per hour. This triggers median nerve compression and tendinitis in the flexor carpi radialis.

  • Vertical Mouse: Neutral "handshake" position.
  • Split Keyboards: Aligned with shoulder width.
  • Articulated Arms: Precise screen height tuning.
  • Pomodoro Breaks: 5-min breaks every 25 mins.

2. Academic & Schools

Students & Teachers
Protecting Youth and Students

Students regularly work on laptops in unadjustable setups, carrying overloaded backpacks that cause spinal asymmetry and trapezius strain. Prolonged smartphone use also fosters "Text Neck".

  • Laptop Risers: Folding devices to elevate screens.
  • 10% Weight Rule: Max backpack capacity limit.
  • Active Pauses: 3-min group stretches mid-class.
  • Text Neck Fix: Holding smartphones at eye level.

3. Industrial Manufacturing

Assembly Lines
Ergonomic Engineering

Cyclic twisting and high-force grip using handheld tools in assembly lines dictate the work rhythm. The continuous repetitive movements easily trigger severe epicondylitis and thoracic outlet syndrome.

  • Job Rotation: Swapping functions every 2 hours.
  • Pneumatic Tools: Balancers provide zero apparent weight.
  • Anti-Fatigue Mats: Stimulating healthy microcirculation.
  • Adjustable Benches: Customizable workstation height.

4. Call Centers & Retail

NR-17 Annex II
High-Volume Data Entry Tasks

Safeguarding high-volume data entry and retail workers as governed by standard ergonomic labor laws. Historically, these sectors suffer from the highest rates of medical leaves due to occupational hazards.

  • Individual Headsets: Freeing both hands for safe typing.
  • Mandatory Breaks: 2x 10-min resting intervals.
  • Checkout Belts: Motorized belts for supermarket cashiers.
  • Humane Quotas: Banning abusive processing targets.

5. E-Sports & Musicians

Fine Motor Strain
Electronic Athletes and Performers

Competitive athletes and instrumentalists executing up to 400 actions per minute (APM), risking De Quervain's tenosynovitis and potentially facing career-ending injuries before reaching their thirties.

  • Articular Warm-up: Heating pads prior to intense gaming.
  • Mouse DPI Sensibility: Balanced optical calibration.
  • Thumb Stretching: Focus on joystick stress relief.
  • Post-Session Cryotherapy: Ice on fatigued tendons.

6. Occupational Therapy

Corporate Risk Auditing
Scientific Assessment Methodologies

Corporate ergonomic risk auditing utilizing validated scientific assessment methodologies designed to lower legal liabilities while prioritizing total employee physical welfare.

  • RULA Method: Focused on upper limb risk assessment.
  • REBA Method: Comprehensive entire body assessment.
  • Corporate Gymnastics: Scheduled routine stretching.
  • Preventive Mapping: Regular tracking of pain symptoms.
